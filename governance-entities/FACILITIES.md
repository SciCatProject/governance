# Facilities

## Abbreviations

AF = Adopting Facility
CF = Contributing Facility

## Definition

A facility is any entity, institute, or group of people that has an active interest in the Project.

An _adopting facility_ is a facility that has installed SciCat in production and SciCat is part their technical infrastructure and data management. New facilities adopting SciCat are encouraged to contact the Project Leaders for recognition as adopting facilities.

A _contributing facility_ is a facility that has allocated at least half of an FT to actively contributing to the Project in any shape or form.

## Responsibilities

There are no official responsibilities for an adopting facility, although, at times, they are called to provide feedback to the project.

A contributing facility has the responsibility to allocate resources equivalent to a half of an FT to sustain and contribute to the Project. Only contributing facilities participate in the [steering committee](STEERING-COMMITTEE.md).
